Who we are

Integrated electrical and mechanical solutions for the downtown core.

Since 1989, Raylan Electrical Systems has provided electrical construction and maintenance, integrated automation and building controls, fire and security systems, structured communication cabling, and standby power to clients across downtown Toronto.

The company was founded by the late Michael Murray on the principles of integrity, reliability and professionalism — values that continue to define how every project is delivered today.

Raylan is owned and led by an experienced management team, and continues to grow on the strength of the people carrying that work forward.
